# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, product leaders, and businesses that need a capable technology team to define, design, build, or improve software. They need to understand WUSLA quickly, see credible evidence, identify where WUSLA can help, and start a focused conversation.

## Product Purpose

WUSLA is an independent software company. It partners with companies as an embedded product and engineering team, and it develops original software applications of its own. The website must turn attention into confidence by showing that WUSLA brings judgment, craft, and ownership to software that needs to work in the real world.

## Brand Personality

Precise, inventive, accountable. The voice is direct and human. The experience should feel technically sophisticated without resembling a SaaS dashboard, a freelancing marketplace, or a decorative agency template.

## Anti-references

- Static brochure sites with repetitive section templates.
- Equal card grids, generic bento layouts, glass panels, gradient text, and fake product dashboards.
- 1990s print pastiche, performative editorial styling, and tiny technical labels used as decoration.
- Motion added for spectacle rather than explanation, feedback, or progression.
- Claims without visible proof, invented metrics, and undisclosed work presented as if it were public.

## Design Principles

- Demonstrate the difference between generated output and software worth releasing.
- Lead with one clear idea, then earn trust through real work.
- Let interaction explain capability instead of adding paragraphs.
- Give every movement a narrative or feedback purpose.
- Keep the path to a project conversation obvious at every decisive moment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, 44px touch targets, responsive reflow, and a complete reduced-motion experience. No important information or action may depend on hover alone.
