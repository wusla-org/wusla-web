# WUSLA Homepage Conversion Prototype

## Scope

Replace only the homepage experience. Preserve every other route, the global navigation, the footer, the existing emerald identity, and the current project data. Do not deploy.

## Conversion Sequence

1. Attention: “Anyone can generate. We make it worth shipping.”
2. Proof: One substantial BeWingo India story using the real project image and concrete decisions.
3. Relevance: An interactive service selector framed around the visitor's situation.
4. Trust: Show connected responsibility from product direction through release, without invented metrics or testimonials.
5. Conversion: One unmistakable “Start a project” action supported by a direct email alternative.

## Visual Direction

Use a release-gate metaphor through cropping, focus, and controlled image treatment. Raw material begins diffuse and becomes precise. The page remains a single emerald-led light theme. Avoid 3D, fake interface imagery, identical cards, excessive labels, and decorative scroll effects.

## Motion Language

- Hero: a user-controlled comparison reveals a real shipped interface from an intentionally degraded draft state.
- Proof: image scale and copy progression respond to scroll to connect decisions with the delivered work.
- Services: selecting, focusing, or hovering a situation changes one shared evidence area with a short directional transition.
- CTA: a restrained mask and arrow response confirms the action without delaying it.
- Reduced motion: all content is immediately available; comparison remains operable; scroll choreography becomes static.

## Responsive Behavior

Desktop uses asymmetric split compositions. Tablet reduces motion distance and preserves the comparison. Mobile becomes a direct single-column story, replaces hover dependence with tap selection, keeps controls at least 44px, and avoids pinned scroll regions.

## Evaluation

- Would a new visitor continue after three seconds?
- Can they understand WUSLA without reading a wall of text?
- Does the prototype prove software and interaction skill?
- Is the route to a conversation unmistakable?

## Boundaries

No other page redesign. No new 3D dependency. No deployment. No invented client results. No changes to route slugs, navigation labels, or form fields.
